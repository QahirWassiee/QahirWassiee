Privacy Policy

Your documents

When you open a PDF, it's read and edited inside your browser. To let you pick up where you left off, the open document and your edits are saved in your browser's own storage on your device; choose Close in the editor to remove them.

When you download, the file and your edits are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, turned into the format you chose in memory and sent straight back. We don't save, log, read or share document contents.

Account data

We store your email address, a securely hashed password and your subscription status. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your account

Email us to delete your account and the data linked to it.
