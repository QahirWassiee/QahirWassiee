Built so your document never shifts

Most PDF editors rebuild the page when they open it, and that's what makes text jump around. PDF 4 Edit leaves your original page exactly as it is and changes only the words you edit.

Edit any text

Layout locked in place

Tables, columns, logos and lines are never re-flowed. Numbers in right-aligned columns stay right-aligned when you change them.

Questions

Will editing move the rest of my document?

No. Your original page stays exactly as it is. Only the text you change is replaced, at the same position and size. Everything else, including every figure you don't touch, stays the same.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Upload a PDF and make all the edits you like for free. You only need a subscription to download.

What if my PDF is a scan or a photo?

Scanned pages are images, so there's no text to click. Use the Erase tool to remove what you want to change, then Add text to type the replacement.

How does the Word conversion work?

Each page becomes a Word page with your graphics kept in the background and every line of text as editable text, positioned where it was in the PDF.

How do I cancel?

Open the editor, click your account and choose Manage billing. You can cancel in one click and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Are there any rules on what I can edit?

Use PDF 4 Edit for documents you're entitled to change. Altering bank statements, pay slips, IDs or official letters to mislead someone is fraud, and it isn't allowed under our terms.
